# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Tracepoint welcomes contributors regardless of experience, background, identity, or location. We expect respectful, constructive collaboration and a focus on evidence, technical reasoning, and safe engineering.

## Expected behavior

- Be professional and considerate in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Explain technical objections with concrete reasoning and actionable suggestions.
- Welcome newcomers and credit others for their work.
- Respect confidentiality and do not disclose security reports or private data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are prohibited.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer using a non-public contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive allegations or private details in issues. The maintainer may moderate comments, close threads, or restrict participation in proportion to the conduct involved.

Security vulnerabilities are handled separately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
